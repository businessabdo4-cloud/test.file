## Specs shown on screen
All from the City Store script / VO (RAM, storage, colours, 5G + SIM, Tab Pen included):
- Galaxy Tab A9+ 5G: 4GB RAM, 64GB, 5G (SIM card)
- Galaxy Tab A11: 4GB RAM, 64GB, Gray
- Lenovo Idea Tab Plus: 6GB RAM, 128GB, Tab Pen in the box
- Xiaomi Pad 8: 8GB RAM, 128GB, Blue; "la plus puissante" = most powerful of these four (per script)

## Drawn elements
Line icons (SIM, signal, book, play, pen, trophy, briefcase, feather) and the pen scribble are original vector drawings.
