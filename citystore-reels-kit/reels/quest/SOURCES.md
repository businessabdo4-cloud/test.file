## Specs shown on screen (from the City Store script, consistent with Meta's Quest 3 spec sheet)
- 512 Go storage
- 4K+ display (Meta's "4K+ Infinite Display" naming)
- Full-colour mixed reality (colour passthrough)
- Standalone: no PC, no cables
