# Episode 2 "الكورة قد النملة" (iPhone Duo): sources

## Voice-over
`assets/duo/vo/voiceover.wav`, recorded dialogue supplied by City Store (23.6 s, 24 kHz mono, -17.9 LUFS as delivered).
Split into 8 lines by silence detection, confirmed by timbre (Hamza ~1240-1500 Hz, Citybot ~1600-1930 Hz spectral
centroid). Whisper could not run in this session (huggingface.co blocked); word timings are syllable-based estimates.
Citybot's lines (L2, L4, L6, L7): light robot effect (+0.5 semitone formant-preserving, 55 Hz ring-mod at 18%, 9 ms comb);
clean versions kept in `public/duo/audio/vo_clean/`.
