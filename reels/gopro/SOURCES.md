| File | Use |

| `assets/gopro/products/src/hero13_black_angle.webp` | used everywhere (transparent; cropped to alpha, enhanced x2: Lanczos + unsharp + slight contrast) |

## Specs shown on screen (all from the City Store script, consistent with GoPro's HERO13 Black spec sheet)
- 5.3K video, 27 MP photos
- HyperSmooth stabilisation
- Waterproof to 10 m without a housing
- Built-in GPS (speed, route)
- Magnetic latch mount (on / off in a second)

## Drawn elements
Adventure tiles, phone, viewfinder landscape, water, GPS map/route and the magnetic mount are original vector drawings.
