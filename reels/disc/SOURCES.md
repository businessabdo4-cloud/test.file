## Specs / claims shown on screen
From the City Store script; consistent with Sony's Disc Drive product page:
- For PS5 Slim Digital Edition and PS5 Pro (on-screen: "Compatible PS5 Slim Digital & PS5 Pro"; the original launch-model PS5 Digital is not compatible)
- Reads PS5 and PS4 discs, plays Ultra HD Blu-ray (4K)
- One-time internet connection required to pair the drive at installation (footnote on screen)
